Privacy Policy for MoneyWise: Expense & Budget

Application: MoneyWise: Expense & Budget Developer: ANGS Corp Package: com.angs.moneywise Last Updated: September 19, 2026

1. Overview & Privacy Philosophy

Welcome to MoneyWise: Expense & Budget (“we”, “our”, or “the App”), developed and published by ANGS Corp.

MoneyWise is a smart, offline personal budgeting and expense tracking utility. We believe that your personal financial records are entirely private. MoneyWise does not require bank logins, does not link to third-party financial data aggregators, and does not operate centralized database servers holding your income, expenditures, or account balances.

100% Local-First Bookkeeping: All accounts, expense categories, transactions, recurring bills, receipt images, and financial goals are processed and stored exclusively on your device hardware.

2. Financial Data Handled & Storage Protections

MoneyWise processes only the financial records you voluntarily record:

  • Account Profiles: Custom account names, currency choices, and manual balance tallies (no real bank account numbers or online banking credentials are ever collected or required).
  • Transaction History: Income, expense amounts, timestamps, tags, payee notes, and categories.
  • Budget Targets & Goals: Monthly budget thresholds and savings target progress.
  • Receipt Attachments: Digital photographs of receipts attached to transactions, stored directly in the app's local sandbox.

All records are stored locally in the isolated application database on your device and are never transmitted to developer servers.

3. Device Permissions and Usage

Permission Purpose Data Handling
Camera Used to capture photos of receipts directly into transaction records. Processed strictly on-device; never uploaded.
Photos & Storage Used to select receipt images or save exported financial spreadsheets (CSV, PDF, JSON). Saved exclusively to your local device.
Notifications Delivers on-device budget threshold alerts and recurring bill reminders. Scheduled locally via device alarm managers; no remote push tracking.
Biometrics Optional fingerprint or Face ID lock to protect access to your financial records. Handled entirely by the operating system keystore; the app never sees raw biometrics.

4. Third-Party Services

MoneyWise operates core financial management completely offline. Third-party integration is strictly confined:

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

  • MoneyWise displays advertisements provided by Google Mobile Ads.
  • Consent Management: We integrate Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for GDPR and CCPA compliance.
  • Complete Separation: The advertising SDK runs in an isolated container and has zero access to your transactions, account balances, budgets, or receipt attachments.

You retain full sovereignty over your financial logs:
