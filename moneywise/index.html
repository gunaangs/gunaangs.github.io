2. Financial Data Handled & Storage Protections

MoneyWise processes only the financial records you voluntarily record or import:

  • Account Profiles: Custom account names, currency choices, and manual balance tallies (no real bank account numbers or online banking credentials are ever collected or required).
  • Transaction History: Income, expense amounts, timestamps, tags, payee notes, and categories.
  • Budget Targets & Flexible Cycles: Monthly, weekly, and bi-weekly budget cycles and savings target progress.
  • Debt Payoff Simulator: Loan balances, interest rates, minimum payments, and payoff schedules (Snowball and Avalanche strategies) calculated purely on-device.
  • Receipt & Bill Attachments: Digital photographs of receipts and invoices attached to transactions, stored directly in the app's local sandbox.
  • Bank Statement CSV Imports: Imported transaction statements parsed on-device using our smart column-mapping wizard with automated duplicate detection.

All records are stored locally in the isolated application SQLite database on your device and are never transmitted to developer servers.

3. Device Permissions & On-Device Intelligence

Permission Purpose Data Handling
Camera Used to capture photos of receipts or bills directly for transaction records and the AI Bill & Receipt Scanner. Processed strictly on-device; images are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud.
Photos & Storage / Media Used to pick receipt images, import bank CSV statements, or save exported financial reports (CSV, PDF, JSON). Saved and processed exclusively within your local device storage.
Notifications Delivers on-device budget threshold alerts and recurring bill due-date reminders. Scheduled locally via device alarm managers; no remote push servers or device tracking.

MoneyWise operates core financial management completely offline. Third-party integration is strictly confined:

5. Data Retention, Portability & User Control

You retain full sovereignty over your financial logs:

  • Data Portability: Export your financial history at any time in open formats (CSV, PDF, JSON).
  • Database Reset: Clear your database or reset individual accounts at any time from Settings.
  • Local Encrypted Backup: You can export an encrypted database backup file (.mwbackup) or financial reports to your device storage. MoneyWise operates 100% offline with zero cloud servers or cloud sync.
  • Complete Erasure on Uninstall: Deleting the app immediately destroys the local database and sandbox records.
