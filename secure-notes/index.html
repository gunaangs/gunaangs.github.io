2. Data We Handle and How It Is Protected

The application processes and stores the following types of information strictly on your local device:

2.1 Encrypted Vault Information

  • Personal Notes: Rich text note content, headers, lists, tags, and folders.
  • Credentials & Passwords: Account titles, usernames, passwords, categories, and custom secure fields.
  • Financial Records: Accounts, expenses, income transactions, recurring entries, budgets, and receipt images.
  • Secure Media Gallery: Encrypted private photos, audio notes, and video files.

2.2 Storage & Encryption Safeguards

  • High-Grade Local Encryption: All vault data and media files are strongly encrypted at rest before storage.
  • Hardware-Backed Security: Cryptographic keys are protected using your device's native secure hardware storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).
  • Volatile Memory Management: Decrypted plaintext in memory is temporary and is immediately wiped upon screen lock, backgrounding, or session timeout.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Security

Permission Purpose Handling
Biometrics (Face ID / Fingerprint) Enables fast, secure biometric vault unlock. Authentication is handled entirely by your operating system. The app never accesses raw biometric data.
Camera Used solely to capture photos or videos directly into the encrypted vault or take receipt photos. Encrypted immediately and saved locally in the app sandbox. Never uploaded.
Microphone & Audio Used to record encrypted voice notes within the media gallery. Saved locally in encrypted format. Never transmitted off-device.
Storage & Documents Used to import/export encrypted backup files upon your explicit instruction. Handled exclusively locally on your device.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

4.1 Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Secure Notes displays advertisements provided by Google Mobile Ads to support continued free development. Consent is managed via Google's UMP framework. The advertising SDK runs in an isolated container and has zero access to your notes, passwords, financial ledgers, or media.

4.2 Zero Cloud Sync & 100% Offline Vault

Secure Notes does not operate or connect to any cloud synchronization, external database, or remote backup servers. All notes, credentials, and encrypted backups reside exclusively on your physical device hardware.

5. Data Retention & User Control

  • Export: Export your data at any time in standard formats.
  • Trash & Permanent Purge: Soft-deleted items can be restored or permanently purged.
  • Factory Reset: Clear all data in Settings to wipe all database files, keys, and cache.
  • Uninstallation: Deleting the app immediately destroys all sandbox records and keys.
