Privacy Policy for PixelSqueeze: Compress to KB

Application: PixelSqueeze: Compress to KB Developer: ANGS Corp Package: com.everest.pixelsqueeze Last Updated: September 19, 2026

1. Overview & Core Architecture

Welcome to PixelSqueeze: Compress to KB (“we”, “our”, or “the App”), developed and published by ANGS Corp.

PixelSqueeze is a precision image optimization and compression utility engineered to reduce image file sizes (JPG, PNG, WebP) to exact target kilobyte thresholds. PixelSqueeze is designed strictly on a 100% on-device, local processing architecture.

Zero Cloud Uploads: Every compression algorithm, image resize, color quantization, and EXIF metadata stripping operation occurs strictly within your device hardware's memory. Your personal photos, documents, and images are never transmitted to or processed on developer servers or remote cloud APIs.

2. Data Handled & Storage Permissions

PixelSqueeze interacts only with the images you explicitly pick for compression:

Permission / Resource How It Is Used Transmission & Custody
Photos / Media Library Allows you to select images from your gallery to be compressed. Never uploaded. Read directly into local app memory for compression.
Storage Save / Write Allows you to save compressed images to your local Downloads or Pictures folder. Saved exclusively to your device storage. We have zero remote access to saved outputs.
EXIF & Geolocation Data Optional metadata sanitization strips camera models, timestamps, and GPS coordinates. Stripped locally on-device to enhance your privacy before sharing.
