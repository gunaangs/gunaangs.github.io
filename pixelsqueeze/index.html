Part I: Privacy Policy

1. Overview & Core Architecture

Welcome to PixelSqueeze: Compress to KB (“we”, “our”, or “the App”), developed and published by Angs Corp.

PixelSqueeze is a precision image optimization, resizing, document dewarping, and photo editing utility engineered to compress and transform images (JPG, PNG, WebP) to exact kilobyte thresholds. PixelSqueeze is designed strictly on a 100% on-device, local processing architecture.

CRITICAL NOTICE — ZERO DEVELOPER BACKUPS & 100% LOCAL STORAGE ARCHITECTURE:

PixelSqueeze is a 100% offline, local storage-based application. There are NO cloud backups, automated server backups, remote image repositories, or cloud sync services provided by Angs Corp or the developer. All selected photos, edited canvases, compressed outputs, and camera scans reside strictly and exclusively on your physical device hardware.

100% USER RESPONSIBILITY FOR DATA LEAKS, THEFT & DEVICE SECURITY:

Because Angs Corp does not possess, transmit, or back up your images or documents on any server, Angs Corp bears ZERO responsibility or liability for any data leak, image exposure, unauthorized access, or data theft. If any photo loss, leak, or theft occurs because your device is compromised, hacked, infected with malware/spyware, physically lost, stolen, rooted, jailbroken, unlocked, or left unattended, it is 100% the responsibility of the user side and NOT the developer side. You are solely responsible for securing your physical device and preserving original copies of your files.

Zero Cloud Uploads: Every compression algorithm, perspective corner-pin dewarping, Skia photo filter, and EXIF metadata stripping operation occurs strictly within your device hardware's memory. Your personal photos, documents, and images are never transmitted to, inspected by, or stored on developer servers or remote cloud APIs.

2. Data Handled & Storage Permissions

PixelSqueeze interacts only with the images you explicitly pick for editing or compression:

Permission / Resource How It Is Used Transmission & Custody
Photos / Media Library Allows you to select images from your gallery to be compressed, cropped, dewarped, or edited. Never uploaded. Read directly into local app memory for processing.
Storage Save / Write Allows you to save compressed or edited outputs to your local Pictures or Downloads folder. Saved exclusively to your device storage. We have zero remote access to saved outputs.
EXIF & Geolocation Data Optional metadata sanitization strips camera models, timestamps, and GPS coordinates. Stripped locally on-device to protect your privacy before sharing.
Document Dewarp & Canvas Renders perspective correction meshes and freeform canvas sketches locally via GPU-accelerated Skia. Rendered strictly in device GPU/memory; never uploaded.

3. Third-Party Services & Ad Isolation

PixelSqueeze processes images fully offline. Third-party integration is strictly confined to Google Mobile Ads:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): PixelSqueeze displays advertisements provided by Google Mobile Ads.
  • Data Processed: Google may collect advertising identifiers and device metrics for ad serving, measurement, and fraud prevention subject to your consent.
  • Consent Management: We integrate Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain GDPR and CCPA consent.
  • Zero Image Access: The ad SDK runs in an isolated container and has zero access to your selected photos, thumbnails, compressed files, or file paths.

4. User Control & Data Deletion

You have direct and complete control over all image files:

  • Compressed images are saved to your chosen local folder and can be deleted by you at any time via your device's gallery or file manager.
  • Temporary compression cache files are cleared immediately upon application close.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all application sandbox caches and settings.
